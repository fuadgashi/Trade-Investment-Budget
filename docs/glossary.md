# Glossary

[← README](../README.md)

The model's own terms. Each rule is stated only in its own document; this page says what a
word means and where to read more.

| Term | Means | See |
|---|---|---|
| **Trade investment** | what the company spends to win shelf space and volume at its customers | [business.md](business.md) |
| **Accumulated** | what the price sets aside, and what suppliers give back | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#words-and-rates) |
| **Spent** | what was paid or given | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#words-and-rates) |
| **Difference** | Accumulated − Spent | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#words-and-rates) |
| **Rate** | a brand's share of its net sales without VAT that the price sets aside: distribution cost, distribution profit, annual bonus, secondary position | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#words-and-rates) |
| **Marketing coefficient** | what the price leaves per unit after cost and the four rates | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#the-price-calculation) |
| **Trade term** | a bonus, placement, campaign or promotion agreed with a customer for an outlet and a brand | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#the-trade-terms) |
| **Term group** | Bonus, Position, Campaign or Promotion | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#the-trade-terms) |
| **Secondary position** | a placement of a brand in a store beyond its shelf | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#secondary-positions) |
| **Bonus customer** | a customer with a bonus term running this year | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#annual-bonus) |
| **Campaign** | a fixed amount agreed with a customer for a brand and a period | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#the-trade-terms) |
| **Promotion** | a fixed amount agreed with a customer for a brand and a period, beyond a campaign | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#the-trade-terms) |
| **Year-End** | a figure projected to 31 December: accumulated with last year's seasonality, position spent as the register's commitment | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#annual-bonus) |
| **Coverage** | how much of the spending the money set aside covers: Total Accumulated ÷ Total Spent | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#total-trade-investment) |
| **Volume effect** | the part of the sales change that came from selling more or fewer units, at last year's price | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#price--volume) |
| **Price effect** | the part of the sales change that came from a higher or lower price, at this year's quantity | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#price--volume) |
| **Verdict** | the pattern behind the change, named by the signs of the two effects | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#price--volume) |
| **Credit note issued** | a credit note to a customer: spend | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#marketing-budget) |
| **Credit note received** | a credit note from a supplier: budget given back | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#marketing-budget) |
| **Service invoice** | an invoice the company sends a supplier for services or rent: budget given back | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#marketing-budget) |
| **Extra discount** | the discount after the regular one, on the invoice line | [trade-investment.md](trade-investment.md#marketing-budget) |
| **Net price** | the selling price after both discounts, with VAT | [measures.md](measures.md#two-sales-bases) |
| **ex VAT** | a sales figure with the VAT of each line taken out | [measures.md](measures.md#two-sales-bases) |
| **Outlet** | a customer's point of sale | [business.md](business.md#customers-and-outlets) |
| **Actual Year** | this year to date, cut at today | [measures.md](measures.md#time-windows-are-functions) |
| **Prior Year YTD** | last year, cut at today's date one year back | [measures.md](measures.md#time-windows-are-functions) |
| **Organisational unit** | a warehouse or van documents are booked in | [model.md](model.md#dimensions) |
